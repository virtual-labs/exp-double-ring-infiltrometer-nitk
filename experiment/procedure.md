### Procedure
These procedure steps will be followed on the simulator.

1. Open the Double Ring Infiltrometer Test and hover over the description to read the definition. Click on the arrow at the bottom-right corner to proceed to the next step.

  ![Alt text](images/1.png)

2. Select one of the given field options and click the arrow at the bottom-right corner to continue.

  ![Alt text](images/2.png)

3. Click on the inner ring to place it on the selected field.

  ![Alt text](images/3.png)

4. Click on the driving plate to place it over the inner ring.

  ![Alt text](images/4.png)

5. Click on the hand icon to hammer the inner ring into the soil using the driving plate.

  ![Alt text](images/5.png)

6. Click on the driving plate to remove it from the top of the inner ring.

  ![Alt text](images/6.png)

7. Answer the pop-up question by selecting the correct option.

  ![Alt text](images/7.png)

8. Click on the arrow at the bottom-right corner to proceed to the next step.

  ![Alt text](images/8.png)

9. Click on the outer ring to place it around the inner ring.

  ![Alt text](images/9.png)

10. Click on the driving plate to place it over the outer ring.

  ![Alt text](images/10.JPG)

11. Click on the hand icon to hammer the outer ring into the soil using the driving plate.

  ![Alt text](images/11.png)

12. Click on the driving plate to remove it from the top of the outer ring.

  ![Alt text](images/12.png)

13. Click on the arrow at the bottom-right corner to proceed to the next step.

  ![Alt text](images/13.png)

14. Click on the plate to place it onto the water level indicator.

   ![Alt text](images/14.png)

15. Click on the water level indicator to place it at the center of the inner ring of the double ring infiltrometer.

    ![Alt text](images/15.png)

16. Click on the arrow at the bottom-right corner to proceed.

    ![Alt text](images/16.png)

17. Click on the bucket to pour water into both rings.

    ![Alt text](images/17.png)

18. Answer the pop-up question by selecting the correct option.

    ![Alt text](images/18.png)

19. Click on the arrow at the bottom-right corner to proceed.

    ![Alt text](images/19.png)

20. Click on the stopwatch to start the timer. Water begins to infiltrate into the soil.

    ![Alt text](images/20.png)

21. Click on the bucket to refill the infiltrometer rings and note down the readings.

    ![Alt text](images/21.JPG)

22. Click on the arrow at the bottom-right corner to proceed to the observation table.

    ![Alt text](images/22.png)

23. Using the observations and the given equations, calculate the infiltration rate and cumulative infiltration. Enter the values in the table and click on the CHECK button to verify the results.

   ![Alt text](images/23.png)

24. Enter the average and accumulated infiltration depth values, then click on the CHECK button. Verify the results by clicking on the RESULTS button.

    ![Alt text](images/24.png)

25. Finally, hover over the inference of the experiment and reload the page to repeat the experiment by selecting another field option.

    ![Alt text](images/25.png)
